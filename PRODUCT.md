# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users
FRC Team 5806 scouts and admins, split roughly evenly between two contexts: scouts on phones in the stands during matches, filling out pit and match scouting forms live in a loud, fast-paced competition environment, often glancing between the field and the screen; and admins (drive team / strategy leads) at a laptop between matches or the night before eliminations, reviewing picklists, assignments, and rolled-up match data to make alliance-selection calls. The same interface has to hold up in both settings without compromising either.

## Product Purpose
A real-time scouting system for competitive robotics events: capture pit and match data accurately and fast enough to keep up with a live event, then surface that data (rankings, EPA, picklists) in a form that makes alliance-selection decisions faster and more confident. Capture and decision-support are equally important — one is useless without the other.

## Positioning
The tool a pit crew actually reaches for mid-event: built for the noise and pace of competition, not for a conference room demo.

## Brand Personality
Precise and fast under pressure, but not sterile — bold and team-spirited, carrying real competition energy. Team colors are maroon and white; the mascot is a lion. The existing "field-ops / pit crew" direction (technical, dense, monospace stat treatment) is the right foundation — the team-spirit side should come through in maroon's confidence and moments of personality (the lion, competition energy), not by softening the utilitarian core into generic dashboard SaaS.

## Anti-references
Generic consumer SaaS dashboards — soft gradients, cute illustrations, cream/pastel backgrounds, rounded-friendly "delightful onboarding" energy. This is a field tool, not a B2B trial signup flow.

## Design Principles
- Built for the stands, not the boardroom: assume bright ambient light, a rushed one-handed scout, and a loud environment — contrast and tap targets should be generous well beyond AA-minimum, not just technically compliant.
- Data reads like telemetry: numbers, ranks, and stats stay legible and scannable at a glance (the existing monospace stat treatment is core to this, not decorative).
- Maroon carries identity and team spirit; graphite carries the everyday interface chrome. Don't let brand energy dilute the density scouts rely on to move fast.
- One system, two contexts: every screen has to work equally well thumbed on a phone mid-match and reviewed carefully on a laptop the night before eliminations.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA as the floor, with contrast pushed generously above minimum given high-glare outdoor/convention-center lighting conditions scouts regularly work in.
